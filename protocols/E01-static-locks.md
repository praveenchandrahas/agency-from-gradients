# E01 — Static locked gradients

## Question
Does heritable variation and selection sustain access to a static, kinetically locked reaction pathway better than passive, fixed-catalyst and faithful-copying, no-new-mutation controls?

## Arms
N, M, D, L, U, A, plus Mmatched, Dmatched, Amatched and Umatched. D and L use paired seeds and identical initial key libraries. D has no new mutation but can select among standing variation; L adds ongoing mutation. U is an idealized designer ceiling. A is a bounded non-replicating adaptive-search comparator.

## Factors
Barrier B = 0, 4, 8, 12 kT; smooth key landscape in the initial run. The key length, catalytic gain, mismatch scale, population cap, mutation rate and energetic costs require sensitivity analysis.

## Primary outcomes
Late-window net throughput and cumulative free-energy release. Secondary outcomes: entropy production (reaction and exchange separately), population persistence, extinction, catalyst investment, stored energy and key–target distance.

## Required controls
At B=0 all catalyst arms must have the same reaction rate as N. Catalysts must multiply forward/reverse rates equally. Closed reaction equilibrium must be invariant to catalysis. Entropy must not enter reproductive fitness.

## Status
Exploratory v0.1 implementation. Not preregistered. Equal catalyst abundance and equal energetic-investment comparisons remain necessary before confirmatory interpretation.
