# Initial reading priorities

This list is a starting map, not an endorsement of a single thermodynamic theory.

1. Stochastic thermodynamics and local detailed balance: establish reaction affinities, fluxes and entropy production consistently.
2. Chemical reaction network theory and autocatalytic sets: clarify the distinction between catalytic persistence and replication.
3. Evolutionary dynamics and population genetics: inheritance, mutation, selection, drift, extinction and adaptation timescales.
4. Evolution in changing environments: adaptation lag, tracking, bet-hedging and environmental stochasticity.
5. Information theory and biological information: distinguish sequence information, environmental information and adaptive search.
6. Maximum entropy production and maximum power debates: treat as competing, conditional hypotheses rather than universal laws.
