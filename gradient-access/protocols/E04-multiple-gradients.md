# E04 — Multiple interacting gradients

## Question
Does evolutionary access become more consequential when multiple kinetically difficult pathways compete for shared resources?

## Initial model
A stack of three locks with the same nominal barrier in the pilot. This is only a smoke test. A substantive experiment should use heterogeneous barriers, shared substrate pools, lock-specific targets and explicit stoichiometric competition.

## Outcomes
Number/fraction of pathways accessed, per-lock flux, total free-energy release, cumulative entropy, specialisation and diversity of catalytic genotypes.

## Required controls
Keep total feed and total available free energy fixed when increasing the number of locks. Otherwise an apparent gain may simply reflect adding more fuel.
