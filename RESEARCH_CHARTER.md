# Research Charter
## From cosmic energy flows to dissipative organization, life and intelligence

**Status:** Working charter, version 0.1

## 1. Research premise

The Universe's evolution creates and maintains energy gradients: concentrated sources of energy, systems that intercept and transform that energy, and sinks into which energy is ultimately dispersed. Stars and planets provide one important arrangement of these conditions.

This research investigates whether increasingly organized dissipative structures can emerge and evolve within such environments. Under suitable conditions, some structures may develop self-maintenance, replication, adaptation, information processing and agency.

Life and intelligence are approached as phenomena to explain within physical law, not as exceptions to it.

This is a **conditional research hypothesis**, not an established consequence of the second law. The work must identify conditions, mechanisms, constraints and probabilities. It must remain possible that some transitions are rare, contingent, chemistry-dependent or not thermodynamically favoured in the way initially conjectured.

## 2. Primary research questions

**Can persistent planetary free-energy flows give rise to increasingly organized dissipative structures, and under what conditions can these structures evolve information processing, agency and intelligence as mechanisms for exploiting those flows?**

A related question is: **How do the emergence and evolution of such structures change the capture, transformation and eventual dissipation of energy at planetary scale?**

## 3. Five propositions

### P1 — Thermodynamic opportunity
Persistent energy flows and appropriate boundary conditions create opportunities for non-equilibrium organization.

To investigate this, characterize energy and exergy flows, thermodynamic forces, timescales, material constraints and boundary conditions. Energy quantity alone is insufficient; energy quality and conversion pathways matter.

### P2 — Emergence and persistence of organization
Under some conditions, matter may organize into structures that capture energy and use it to maintain their organization.

A structure must be shown to form and persist through identifiable mechanisms, rather than being inserted by initial conditions or imposed by the modeller. Compare persistence with abiotic and non-maintaining controls. A dissipative structure is not automatically alive.

### P3 — Replication, heredity and evolution
Some self-maintaining structures may acquire replication, heredity and variation. Differential persistence and reproduction can then produce evolutionary change without an externally prescribed objective.

Specify how replication becomes possible, how information is transmitted, how variation occurs and how differential survival and reproduction follow from local conditions. The origin of replicators is distinct from evolution after replicators already exist.

### P4 — Evolution of agency
In sufficiently structured or variable environments, sensing, memory, information processing, prediction and action may confer an advantage that exceeds their energetic and material costs.

These capabilities need operational definitions, physically plausible costs and explicit causal pathways to improved decisions or outcomes. They must be selectable traits, not direct bonuses attached to labels such as intelligence.

### P5 — Planetary thermodynamic consequences
Biological and agentic structures may alter planetary energy capture, conversion and dissipation.

Measure these effects independently of individual fitness. Greater energy capture, greater biological throughput, greater free-energy use and greater entropy production are not synonyms. The direction of the planetary effect is an outcome, not an assumption.

## 4. Working causal framework

**Stellar and planetary boundary conditions → persistent free-energy gradients → non-equilibrium organization → self-maintaining structures → replication and heritable variation → natural selection → information processing and agency → possible evolution of intelligence → changes in planetary energy flows.**

This is a scaffold for investigation, not a deterministic law. Each arrow represents a separate causal question. The transition from non-living organization to self-maintaining replication cannot be silently assumed.

## 5. Quantities that must remain distinct

- **Energy:** Conserved physical quantity. Accounting must close within a clearly defined system boundary.
- **Free energy / exergy:** Capacity of energy or matter, relative to specified environmental constraints, to perform useful work.
- **Energy throughput:** Rate at which energy crosses a defined boundary or is transformed within it.
- **Biological throughput:** Energy captured and transformed by biological entities or populations.
- **Maintenance:** Expenditure and processes required to preserve a structure over time.
- **Evolutionary fitness:** Contribution to future generations under a specified population and ecological model. It is not synonymous with energy capture.
- **Entropy production:** Entropy generated by irreversible processes within a specified boundary. Distinguish it from entropy transported across that boundary.
- **Information:** A measurable description of states, correlations or uncertainty. Information processing has physical implementation and costs.
- **Agency:** Operationally defined capacity to use information to select and execute actions that affect future states. This definition must be refined before modelling.

## 6. Thermodynamic and evolutionary separation

Natural selection in the model should operate through the persistence and reproduction of entities or structures. It should not optimize planetary entropy production.

If evolutionary dynamics change global dissipation, those changes should be observed and explained after the fact. They must not be guaranteed by the fitness function.

Any proposed maximum entropy production, maximum power or related organizing principle must be stated as a separate hypothesis, with explicit conditions and competing predictions. It must not be smuggled into the second law.

## 7. Scope and non-claims

This research does not assume:
- The second law requires life, intelligence or increasing complexity.
- All dissipative structures evolve toward greater complexity.
- Greater energy capture always increases entropy production.
- Natural selection maximizes planetary power or entropy production.
- Intelligence is a universal endpoint of evolution.
- A simulation of existing organisms explains abiogenesis.
- A model output establishes a physical mechanism without justified assumptions and correspondence to the physical system.

## 8. Research standard

Major claims should be evaluated using explicit system boundaries and conservation accounting; matched controls and ablations; sensitivity analysis; independent measurement of energy, exergy, fitness and entropy production; alternative mechanisms and null models; replication across model formulations where practical; and clear separation of observations, assumptions, interpretations and speculation.

## 9. Ultimate objective

To investigate whether life, agency and intelligence are contingent but physically intelligible developments in the Universe's thermodynamic evolution, and to identify the mechanisms and conditions that connect them.
