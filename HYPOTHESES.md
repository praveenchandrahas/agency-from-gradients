# Hypotheses and falsifiers

## H0 — Model gates

All conservation, detailed-balance, non-negative entropy-production and numerical-convergence tests must pass before substantive outcomes are interpreted.

## H1 — Barrier dependence

At zero barrier, catalytic arms have no material advantage over the passive baseline. At high barriers, catalysis increases access relative to N.

**Falsifier:** a systematic L advantage at B=0, or failure of catalytic rates to preserve equilibrium.

## H2 — Evolutionary access

At sufficiently high barriers, L sustains greater late-window pathway throughput and accessible free-energy release than fixed random catalysts matched to L's late-window abundance (Mmatched), and than the faithful-copying, no-new-mutation control D. Amatched tests generic adaptive search at the same catalyst abundance.

**Falsifier:** paired intervals for L−Mmatched or L−D include zero or favour the non-evolving arm across the preregistered cold regime.

## H3 — Selection versus adaptation

Compare L with oracle-assisted, bounded adaptive search A and its matched-size version Amatched. If Amatched matches L at equal total energetic/material cost, the result supports adaptive search rather than a distinctive advantage of evolutionary inheritance.

## H4 — Environmental drift

There is a range of drift rates at which L retains access better than fixed M and faithful-copying D. At sufficiently rapid drift, adaptation may fail.

**Falsifier:** no robust region of differential persistence across plausible parameter sensitivity ranges.

## H5 — Multiple locks

L may sustain access to a larger fraction of a multi-lock landscape than non-evolving arms.

**Falsifier:** L does not outperform the best matched non-evolving control in the preregistered conditions.

## H6 — Cumulative dissipation

Evolutionary access may increase cumulative dissipation over a declared horizon, but this is not assumed to follow from higher instantaneous throughput.

**Falsifier:** no cumulative advantage after stored energy, construction and maintenance costs are accounted for.

## Cross-cutting null

Evolutionary search need not outperform fixed, adaptive or non-living alternatives. The model must allow this result.
