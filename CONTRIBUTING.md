# Contributing

This repository is a research programme. Contributions should improve conceptual clarity, physical plausibility, testability or reproducibility.

## Before proposing a change

State:
1. The question or claim being addressed.
2. The relevant system boundary and scale.
3. Definitions of key quantities.
4. Assumptions and known limitations.
5. Relevant literature, including credible competing views.
6. A proposed test, derivation, model or reason the issue is currently unresolved.

## Scientific standards

- Distinguish established laws from hypotheses and interpretations.
- Keep energy, exergy, fitness, throughput and entropy production separate.
- Do not present maximum entropy production as a universal law.
- Do not use a simulation's assumptions as evidence for its conclusions.
- Include null results and results that contradict the working hypothesis.
- Prefer small, interpretable models with clear accounting over large opaque simulations.
- Document units, parameter provenance, uncertainty and numerical methods.

## Pull requests

A useful pull request should explain what changed and why, which hypothesis or roadmap stage it supports, how the change can be tested or reviewed, and what it does not establish.

Discussion and disagreement are welcome when claims are precise and evidence-based.
