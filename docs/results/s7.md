# S7: bigger genomes, many more generations

Preregistered in [`PREREGISTRATION_S7.md`](https://github.com/praveenchandrahas/agency-from-gradients/blob/main/PREREGISTRATION_S7.md). 20 seeds per arm, 200,000 steps. Raw runs are on the [`ci-results` branch](https://github.com/praveenchandrahas/agency-from-gradients/tree/ci-results).

!!! abstract "Verdict"
    **H7a, H7b and H7c: not supported.** The combined package did not work. Most big-genome runs went extinct.

## The objection I was answering

A few dozen genes is too small. Agency will not evolve from that.

Fair. So I built a bigger world, a bigger genome, and gave it far more generations. The [pilots](../world-v2.md) came first. They did not look promising. The preregistered run was the proper test.

## What the package bundled

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Recombination (block crossover) | Mix good blocks across parents |
| A 5x lower mutation rate | Cut the harmful mutations per child |
| A 3x larger arena | More individuals, more mutational supply |
| A bigger genome, about 5,300 genes | Room for a real policy |
| A richer world | Day and night, a drifting resource field, wandering danger zones |

## The four arms

| Arm | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A** | Recurrent policy, about 5,300 genes, **persistence selection** (the treatment) |
| **B** | Same genome, **random parents** (no selection) |
| **C** | The 29-gene policy, same world |
| **D** | The hand-coded forager. Headroom only, not tested |

## Results

| Arm | Tier-2 share | Entropy rate | Generations | Extinct before 200k steps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0.000 | 112 | about 1,500 | **90%** |
| B | 0.000 | 117 | about 1,600 | 70% |
| C | 0.037 [0.001, 0.090] | 198 | about 1,800 | 20% |
| D | 0.399 [0.295, 0.501] | 201 | about 3,000 | 20% |

| Hypothesis | Contrast | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **H7a** | A minus B | -0.000 [-0.001, -0.000] | Not supported |
| **H7b** | A minus C | -0.037 [-0.092, -0.001] | Not supported. **Sign reversed** |
| **H7c** | A, last tenth minus first tenth | -0.020 [-0.033, -0.008] | Not supported |

## What to take from it

**Selection did not help the large genome.** Arms A and B are indistinguishable on entropy rate, generations and extinction. The extinction gap, 90% against 70%, has overlapping intervals.

**The big caveat: extinction.** Most A and B populations died out. Their metrics come from truncated runs. Extinct runs were included as they ended. My preregistration did not say how to handle them. That is a gap, and I logged it.

**v3 is harsh.** Even the hand-coded forager lost 20% of its runs. For a prior-free genome, the world is close to unsurvivable.

So here is what the test does and does not show.

- It **does** show this package fails at this budget.
- It **does not** show whether a big genome could evolve ladder use in a gentler world.

**And I bundled five changes.** So even a positive result could not have been pinned on any one of them.

## What I have not tried

- Seeding with a viable population.
- An easier world.
- A longer survival grace period.
- A curriculum from v1 to v3.

Any of these would be a new preregistered test. Not a tweak to this one.
